The web version has been retired. Lia Field is now an installed app on iOS and Android, which is what lets it read NFC tags and keep your equipment list on the phone.

Getting the app

Your supervisor has the install link for your phone.

Once it is installed, sign in with your work account and let it download the equipment list once. After that it works with no signal.

Checking a certificate

Looking up a ladder or a piece of fall-protection equipment does not need the app — the certificate site still works in any browser.
