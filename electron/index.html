How Lia Office is used

1 · A CSV from one tech

  1. Office Mode → Choose CSV. The file a tech exported with Share CSV.
  2. Enter the work order number. Required: it is what the import is billed against — one token per work order, however many times you run it — and the only thing tying this import to what the techs recorded.
  3. Review the parsed ladders and parts, then Open Browser.
  4. Sign in to BSI yourself and open the work order. Lia waits: nothing on the page reliably says which work order is open, so a person confirms it.
  5. Lia reads what is already on the work order and offers to add everything, only what is missing, or to stop.

2 · What one tech recorded in the field

No CSV, no email. Their work is already here.

  1. Merge Field Work. Work orders appear as techs upload, with who contributed to each.
  2. Open one. You get every record, with the parts they tapped.
  3. Import this into BSI — it becomes an import with the work order already filled in, and runs exactly as a CSV does.

3 · Several techs on one work order

Same screen. Two techs on one job is the normal case, not an exception.

  • Newest capture wins when two techs recorded the same serial — except that a FAIL always beats a PASS. A condemnation is evidence, not an opinion, so there is no button to override it.
  • Disagreements are listed with both values, so you can see what was decided for you.
  • A record a tech deleted after uploading is left out, and named.
  • If a phone's clock is well out, you are told which records "newest wins" used it on.

Then Import this into BSI, once, for the merged set.

4 · Check the work after every import

This is the step people skip. An import that finishes is not an import that put everything on the work order, and the summary is where the difference shows.

  • Read the run summary. It says how many boxes were added and which ladders were skipped — usually because they were already on the work order, or because BSI would not accept a value.
  • Cost flags. Boxes with PM36 over $90, and any box over $250, are called out. They are not errors; they are the ones worth looking at before the customer does.
  • Compare the count with what the tech recorded. If the tech has 40 and the work order has 38, the two skipped ones are named in the summary.
  • Run this again is safe: it re-reads the work order and offers only what is still missing. It never adds a second copy of a box it already put there.
  • The full log is kept in Documents/Lia Logs.

5 · Something wrong, or something missing?

Tell us here. It reaches the developer with your account and version attached, and you can be replied to. Techs can do the same from the phone app under ?.

Your Crew

Invite a field person
They get an email, set their own password, and appear on this list. Nobody needs a Supabase login for this. Sending again to somebody who already has an account resends the link — that is how you replace one that expired or was deleted.
A field person carries no technician number: the certificate names the lead of this account, whoever did the inspection. They get the phone app, not Lia Office.
Subcontractors
Companies under you
Take on a subcontractor
A new company with its own equipment, work orders and crew. They see none of your other subcontractors, and none of them see this one. Their lead gets an email and sets their own password.
The technician number goes on every certificate their company issues, so it cannot be left blank. Credits are billed to them; 0 blocks imports until you set a balance.
