Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Scope

This policy covers the Lia Field mobile application for iOS and Android (com.mobileappdevelopmentgroup.liafield), published by Mobile App Development Group. It does not cover Lia Office, our separate desktop application, which operates under a different data model.

Data We Collect

None. Lia Field does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics.

How Your Information Is Stored

Everything you enter into Lia Field — ladder serial numbers, location IDs, part selections, inspection notes, and job records — is stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. This information is never uploaded to our servers or to any third party. We have no ability to access it.

You can remove all stored data at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. Once removed, it is permanently gone; because we never receive a copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our end.

Camera

Lia Field requests camera access for a single purpose: scanning barcodes on ladder serial-number tags. Barcode decoding happens entirely on your device, in real time.

  • No photos or video are saved to your device by the app.
  • No images are transmitted off your device, to us or to anyone else.
  • Camera access is optional — you can decline it and enter serial numbers manually.

Network Access

The packaged mobile app operates entirely offline. All of its code and assets, including the barcode-scanning library, are bundled inside the app. Lia Field makes no network requests during normal operation.

Third-Party Services

None. Lia Field contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no social or tracking integrations.

Children's Privacy

Lia Field is a professional tool for ladder-inspection technicians and is not directed at children. Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how Lia Field handles data, we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
