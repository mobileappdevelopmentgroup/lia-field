Lia Field
Privacy
Lia Field is a work tool for inspection technicians. It collects the inspection records you create, and the account you sign in with — nothing else.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Scope

This policy covers the Lia Field mobile application for iOS and Android (com.mobileappdevelopmentgroup.liafield), published by Mobile App Development Group. Lia Field is issued to technicians by the company that employs or contracts them; it is not a consumer app and has no public sign-up.

Data We Collect

Only what the app needs to do its job:

  • Your account — the email address and name on the account your employer creates for you, and your technician (rep) number.
  • Inspection records — work order numbers, equipment serial numbers, manufacturer, model, lot and manufacture dates, inspection results, and any notes you add.
  • Photographs you take of defective equipment, which are required as evidence when an item is removed from service.
  • Who recorded what, and when — the account that captured each record, a device identifier, and timestamps. This is so the lead technician on a job can see which technician submitted which items.

We do not collect location or GPS data, contacts, advertising identifiers, or any information about how you use your phone outside this app.

How Your Information Is Stored and Shared

Records are stored on your device first, so the app works with no signal, and are uploaded to our servers when you are back on a connection. Your employer's account can see the records captured under it — that is the purpose of the app.

Equipment inspection results are also published to a public certificate page, so that anyone holding a piece of equipment can check whether it is safe to use. That page shows the equipment's serial number, manufacturer and model, inspection and due dates, the responsible technician's rep number, and whether it passed. It does not show photographs, the reason an item was condemned, or which individual technician collected the record.

Where It Is Processed

Our servers and database are hosted by Supabase, which stores and processes this information on our behalf. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else except where the law requires it.

How Long We Keep It

  • Photographs of condemned equipment: 2 years, then deleted automatically.
  • Inspection records: kept for as long as your employer's account is active, because an equipment safety certificate has to remain verifiable.
  • Your account: until your employer removes it.

Camera

Lia Field uses the camera for two things:

  • Scanning barcodes on equipment tags. Decoding happens on your device; the image is not kept or uploaded.
  • Photographing defective equipment, when you remove an item from service. These photos are uploaded, because they are the evidence for that decision.

You can decline camera access and enter serial numbers by hand — but a photograph is required to record an item as defective.

NFC

Where your device supports it, Lia Field reads and writes NFC tags attached to equipment, so a technician can identify an item by holding the phone against it. Only the tag's identifier and the equipment serial number are involved. NFC is optional; the app works without it.

Third-Party Services

Lia Field contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, and no social or tracking integrations. Supabase, named above, is our hosting and database provider and acts only on our instructions.

Your Rights

You can ask for a copy of the information held about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by writing to the address below. Because these records are your employer's business records, requests to delete inspection data are handled with them.

Children's Privacy

Lia Field is a professional tool issued to working technicians. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes to This Policy

If we change how Lia Field handles data, we will update this page and revise the date above. Material changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
