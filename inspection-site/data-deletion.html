Data Deletion
You can request deletion of your Lia Field account and the personal data held about you at any time. You do not need the app installed to make a request.

Requesting Deletion of Your Data

Applies to: Lia Field (com.mobileappdevelopmentgroup.liafield) · Last updated: August 24, 2026

How to Request

Email admin@mobileappdevelopmentgroup.com from the email address associated with your Lia Field account, with the subject “Lia Field data deletion request”.

Send a deletion request

We confirm receipt within 5 business days and complete verified requests within 30 days. We may ask one follow-up question to confirm you control the account before acting, so that nobody can delete someone else’s records.

What Gets Deleted

DataOn request
Your account — name, email address, technician (rep) numberDeleted
Your sign-in credentials and sessionsDeleted
The link between you and the records you collectedRemoved — records are anonymised
Photographs you took of condemned equipmentDeleted, unless an item is still out of service and the photo is its evidence

What Is Kept, and Why

Equipment inspection records are not deleted on an individual request. They are safety certificates. A ladder or harness in active service has to stay verifiable by whoever is about to stand on it, and the record belongs to the company that employs or contracts you, not to us.

After a deletion request, those records remain but are no longer connected to you as an individual. The public certificate page never showed your name — it shows the responsible technician’s rep number, the equipment details, and the result.

To have the inspection records themselves deleted, the request has to come from the account holder — the company the account was issued by. Lia Field is a professional tool with no public sign-up; accounts are created by employers. If you are that account holder, say so in your email and we will handle the whole account, records included.

Automatic Deletion

Some data is removed on a schedule with no request needed:

  • Photographs of condemned equipment — deleted automatically after 2 years.
  • Your account — removed when your employer removes it.
