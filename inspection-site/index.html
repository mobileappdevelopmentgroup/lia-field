How to use this page

For site and field managers checking ladders and fall-protection equipment before use.

1

Find the number

Every inspected item carries a tag. Scan its QR code, tap it with your phone, or read the number printed on it.

2

Search it

Type the serial, the tag number or the work order into the box above. No account or app needed.

3

Act on the result

The page tells you whether the item can be used and exactly what to do next.

What each result means

✓ Valid

OK to use. Inspected and passed; the date shows when the next inspection is due. Still check it over before each use.

! Due soon

OK to use for now. Its inspection falls due within 30 days — book it before the date shown.

! Overdue

Take it out of use until it is re-inspected. It may be fine, but nobody has checked it inside the required period.

✕ Failed

Do not use. It failed inspection and was removed from service. The failed checks are listed on its record.

✕ Destroyed

Do not use. The ladder was not worth repairing and was cut and destroyed at inspection. If you find it in use, remove it and report the serial.

? No record

We have no inspection for that number. Check it was typed exactly as printed, or try the tag number. If it still isn't found, treat the item as not inspected.

Common questions

How do I get the records for a whole job?

Search the work order number from your invoice or service paperwork. You'll get every item on that job with its result, anything removed from service listed first, and buttons to print it, save it as a PDF or download a spreadsheet.

Where is the serial number?

Ladders: on the manufacturer's label on a side rail, usually near the bottom.

Harnesses, lanyards and lifelines: on the sewn-in label or the housing. If the item has an inspection tag, the tag number (like FP161340) works too.

What is the certificate code?

A 10-character code (like SHJM3W0P8C) that identifies one item's certificate. It's what a tag's QR code or chip carries, so scanning a tag takes you straight to the right record.

Scanning doesn't work

Allow camera access when your phone asks, and hold the code steady about a hand's width away. On iPhone, the normal Camera app can also scan the tag's QR code. If all else fails, type the number printed on the tag.

The record looks wrong, or an item is missing

Contact the company that inspected your equipment and give them the serial number and the work order shown on the record. Corrections are made by them and appear here as soon as they are saved — the original is kept on file.

Can I print or save a certificate?

Yes. Every record and work order has a Print / Save PDF button. On a phone, choose "Save as PDF" in the print screen. You can also copy a link to send to someone.
